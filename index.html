Talonaris Shorts Studio

Operated by Salih Tekin

Talonaris Shorts Studio is an internal, single-operator tool. It prepares original short-form videos and publishes them to two YouTube channels owned by the operator. It is not a public service: no one else can sign in, and it acts on no one else's account.

What it does

  • Uploads the operator's own original videos to the operator's own channels using the YouTube Data API.
  • Schedules each video's publication time.
  • Reads those channels' own analytics (views, watch time) so future videos can be improved.

What it does not do

  • It does not download, copy or re-upload anyone else's content.
  • It does not collect data about viewers, and it does not sell or share any data.
  • It does not access any Google account other than the operator's.
